How to order

  1. Send a message and tell me which commission type you want.
  2. Send character references and the mood you have in mind.
  3. Pay a 50% deposit once the price is agreed.
  4. Receive the sketch, request revisions, then pay the rest and get the final files.

Quick rules

  1. Up to 2 free revisions at the sketch stage.
  2. Personal use is fine. Ask first for commercial use.
  3. I may decline certain content.
  4. Deposits are non-refundable once work has started.
